5. **Help-Meet (HELMET) combines HEL + MET:** The compound structure emphasizes the fusion of support (HEL) with compatibility (MET), creating a unified concept

6. **One Flesh (ONFLE):** The ON prefix creates unity semantics, while FLE (flesh) grounds it in physical reality

7. **Deep Sleep (DESLE):** Uses D-E (boundary descent) + SLE (suspended state) to model consciousness as a boundary that can be lowered
